Editorial standards

Tool Radar distinguishes research-based analysis from genuine hands-on testing. We use terms such as analyzed, compared, scanned, or shortlisted when content is based on public information and automated research. We reserve tested, used, and hands-on for actual firsthand use.

Commercial principles

  • Affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly.
  • Paid relationships do not guarantee positive coverage.
  • Product relevance and usefulness remain part of the selection process.
  • We prefer measurable, performance-based partnerships where possible.
