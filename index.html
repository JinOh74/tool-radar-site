Editorial policy

Research is not the same as hands-on testing.

Trust matters. Tool Radar does not describe a product as personally tested or used unless genuine hands-on testing occurred.

Research-based content

When content is based on public information and automated analysis, we use language such as analyzed, compared, scanned, or shortlisted.

Hands-on content

Terms such as tested, used, or hands-on are reserved for cases where the product was actually used or tested.
